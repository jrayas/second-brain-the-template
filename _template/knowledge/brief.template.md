# Domain brief

Copy this file to `_template/knowledge/<slug>/brief.md` and fill it in. Leave a line blank or write "default" to accept the default. Keep it short; Claude Code works out the sections, tracks, tags, content plan and visuals from it.

- **slug:** (lowercase letters and digits, 2 to 12 characters, for example `iot`)
- **name:** (for example Internet of Things)
- **goal:** (one or two sentences: who the reader is and what they should be able to do at the end)
- **topics:** (8 to 15 topic areas, in the order a learner should meet them, separated by semicolons)
- **tools or platforms to reference:**
- **standards or sources to name:**
- **accuracy risks:** (what is most likely to be wrong or to go out of date)
- **network:** none (default) or official-docs-read-only (reading official documentation only; every source is recorded in `references` with its access date)
- **installs:** none (default) or a list of Python packages
- **review cycle:** default (6-months), or 3-months, 1-month, yearly
- **size target:** default (about 40 kb, 12 example, 20 exercise, 10 scenario and 8 visual pages), or your own numbers (it is a floor: the coverage audit adds more, and every section also gets a lesson page)
- **coverage areas to check:** (optional: sectors, subtypes, failure modes or tools you want covered)
- **safety and scope:** (optional but recommended for medicine, law, finance and anything physical: who is the reader, what must the base never claim or advise, what disclaimer applies)
